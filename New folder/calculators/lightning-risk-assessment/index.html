Project Informationi

Structure Geometryi

Dimensions of the building and any protruding structures, plus the local lightning strike density.
AD = max(Ad1, Ad2), where Ad1 covers the building footprint plus side exposure (height-dependent) and Ad2 the exposure of a chimney / tower. Set T to 0 when none exists. AM surrounds the structure at a 500 m radius.

Risk Factors & Loss Parametersi

Select the structure circumstances, protection measures and services. The Lightning Protection Level (PB) and the SPD system (PSPD) are the two mitigations you can adjust to bring the risk within the tolerable thresholds.

Resultsi

Assessed risks update live as you change the inputs. A risk above its tolerable threshold is shown as NOT ACCEPTABLE. Each row breaks the risk down into its components (RA, RB, RC, RM).
