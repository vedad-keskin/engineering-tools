Electrical design calculators

A small toolkit of standalone, client-side calculators for LV electrical design. Pick a tool below — everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

LV Cable Sizing Calculator

Available
Size LV feeders per IEC 60364: ampacity, derating, voltage drop, earth-fault-loop checks.

Capacity Planner

Available
Build a supply hierarchy (utility → transformers → UPS → panels → loads), roll up demand, and check equipment utilisation.

Block Diagram Builder

Available
Build a supply network and switch to a visual block diagram: drag symbols, reconnect feeds by click, dual-feed P/R labels, dashed redundant paths, and SVG/PNG export.

Network Diagram Builder

Available
Build a supply network (same table-based workflow as Capacity Planner) and get an auto-laid-out single-line block diagram alongside it: drag-to-reposition symbols, draw connections by hand, P/R and HV/LV labelling, undo/redo, autosave, and SVG/PNG/report export.

Earthing Cable Calculator

Coming soon
Size earthing / grounding conductors and verify fault-withstand requirements.

Lightning Risk Assessment

Available
IEC 62305-2:2010 component-based lightning protection risk assessment (RA, RB, RC, RM → R1/R2/R3) versus tolerable thresholds for human life, service, and cultural heritage.

Building PUE Calculator

Coming soon
Estimate data centre / building Power Usage Effectiveness from load inputs.
